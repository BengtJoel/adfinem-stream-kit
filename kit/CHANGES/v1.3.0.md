**Music for the stream, muted just for you.** Setup now sets up a music channel by itself:

- installs **VB-CABLE** (free, by VB-Audio, downloaded from vb-audio.com - Windows asks for permission once) and puts your normal speakers/headset back as default
- sends **Spotify** into it (Windows remembers this per app)
- adds **AF Music** to every AD FINEM scene in OBS, and plays it in your headphones too
- new Stream Deck key **Music** (and hotkey Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F12): turns the music off **only in your headphones** - the stream keeps it. With the OBS plugin the key shows MUSIC / STREAM ONLY.

The stream never gets the music twice: while you listen and Desktop Audio is on, the music reaches the stream through Desktop Audio; when you mute it for yourself, OBS sends it to the stream directly. Turn the option off on Setup's webcam/mic page if you don't want it. Uninstall puts Spotify back on your normal output.
