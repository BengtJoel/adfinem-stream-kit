**Mac support.** The kit now installs on macOS too: unzip it and double-click **INSTALL-mac.command**. It sets up OBS with macOS screen capture for WoW, your webcam and mic (with the clean-up filters), and asks Retail, WoW Forever or both - same as on Windows. The overlay editor opens from "AD FINEM - Customize overlays" on your Desktop; connect Twitch there. See README > MAC.
