**Linux and Mac: the installer always asks which game.** Before, it only asked the first time, so if you already had the kit you could not get the WoW Forever scene collection without knowing the `--both` option. Now `install-linux.sh` / `INSTALL-mac.command` asks every time: Retail, WoW Forever or both (Enter keeps what you have). Pick **both** to get the second scene collection, "AD FINEM Forever".
