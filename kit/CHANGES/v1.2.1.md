**New: Twitch goal on the in-game overlay.** The pill at the top can now show a follower or subscriber goal with a progress bar, for example FOLLOWER GOAL 73 / 100. It counts live from your Twitch channel and moves to the next goal by itself when you reach it (100, 200, 300 ...).

With the WoW Forever look it shows the Twitch goal by default; Retail keeps the raid progress from raider.io. Change it in Customize overlays > In-game > "The pill shows", and set the goal and the text there. It needs Twitch connected on the Alerts tab (also if you use StreamElements or Streamlabs alerts).
