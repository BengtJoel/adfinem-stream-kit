**New setup window.** Installing and updating now happens in a proper AD FINEM setup window instead of a black console.

- **Webcam and microphone setup** - pick your camera and mic in the setup and they're put into OBS for you.
- **Better mic sound** - noise suppression, noise gate, compressor and limiter added to your mic (choose which ones).
- **Webcam placeholder** - the webcam frame shows the AD FINEM picture until your camera is on, or when you hide the camera.
- Updates keep your camera, mic, filters, texts, sponsors and Twitch connection.
